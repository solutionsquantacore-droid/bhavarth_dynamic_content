The 12 Jyotirlingas

The Sacred Columns of Light of Lord Shiva

A Jyotirlinga is a devotional shrine where Lord Shiva appeared as a fiery column of light. There are 12 major Jyotirlingas spread across India, and visiting them is believed to wash away sins and bring salvation.

1. Somnath

📍 Veraval, Gujarat

Considered the first of the 12 Jyotirlingas, Somnath is known as 'The Protector of the Moon God'. The temple has been destroyed and rebuilt multiple times, standing as a symbol of eternal resilience.

2. Mallikarjuna

📍 Srisailam, Andhra Pradesh

Located on the Shri Saila mountain, it is unique as it houses both a Jyotirlinga (Lord Shiva) and a Shakti Peeth (Goddess Bhramaramba).

3. Mahakaleshwar

📍 Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

The only Jyotirlinga facing south (Dakshinamukhi). Famous for its intense 'Bhasma Aarti' where the lord is woken up with sacred ash.

4. Omkareshwar

📍 Khandwa, Madhya Pradesh

Situated on an island in the Narmada river that is naturally shaped like the holy symbol 'Om'.

5. Kedarnath

📍 Rudraprayag, Uttarakhand

Located high in the Himalayas, it is the highest of the Jyotirlingas. It remains covered in snow for six months of the year.

6. Bhimashankar

📍 Pune, Maharashtra

Located in the Sahyadri hills, the river Bhima originates from this sacred forest shrine.

7. Kashi Vishwanath

📍 Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

Situated in the spiritual capital of India. It is believed that a glimpse of this Jyotirlinga brings liberation (Moksha).

8. Trimbakeshwar

📍 Nashik, Maharashtra

Unique for having three faces embodying Lord Brahma, Lord Vishnu, and Lord Rudra. It is located at the source of the Godavari river.

9. Vaidyanath

📍 Deoghar, Jharkhand

Also known as Baba Baidyanath, it is believed that praying here cures all physical and mental ailments.

10. Nageshwar

📍 Dwarka, Gujarat

Known as the protector from poisons, this powerful temple is located on the coast of Saurashtra.

11. Ramanathaswamy

📍 Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu

The southernmost Jyotirlinga. It was established and worshipped by Lord Rama before his journey to Lanka.

12. Grishneshwar

📍 Ellora, Maharashtra

Located near the famous Ellora Caves, it is the last of the 12 Jyotirlingas, signifying the completion of the pilgrimage.

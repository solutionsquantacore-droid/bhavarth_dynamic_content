Maa Kushmanda

Fourth Day of Navratri

The Divine Form of Maa Kushmanda

On the fourth day of Navratri, the fourth power of Goddess Durga, 'Maa Kushmanda', is worshipped and adored. She is called Goddess Kushmanda because she created the 'Anda', meaning the universe, with her gentle and radiant smile. Her divine abode resides within the core of the Sun. Endowed with eight arms, she is gracefully revered as Ashtabhuja Devi. Her mount is the majestic lion.

Hymns and Mantras

Om Devi Kushmandayai Namah॥
Surasampurnakalasham Rudhiraplutameva Cha.
Dadhana Hastapadmabhyam Kushmanda Shubhadastu Me॥

Rituals of Worship (Puja Vidhi)

  • Bathing and Invocation: Bathe in the early morning, wear green attire, and meditate upon the divine form of Maa Kushmanda.
  • Kalash Pujan: First and foremost, offer your prayers to the Kalash deity (Lord Ganesha) and the Navagrahas (Nine Planets).
  • Sacred Offerings: Offer Kumkum, Akshat (unbroken rice), and red or yellow flowers (such as the Lotus) to the Supreme Goddess.
  • Aarti: Light incense and the sacred lamp to perform the Aarti of the Mother, and recite the Durga Chalisa with devotion.

The Mythological Saga

According to mythological legends, when the universe did not exist and there was absolute darkness everywhere, Maa Kushmanda created the entire cosmos with a mere hint of her divine, gentle smile. Hence, she is revered as the primordial creator and the Adishakti (Supreme Power) of the universe.

She resides in the Suryaloka (the realm of the Sun), and only Maa Kushmanda possesses the magnificent power and capacity to dwell within its fiery core. The radiance and glow of her physical form are as resplendent and luminous as the Sun itself. Through her devout worship, all ailments and sorrows of her devotees are forever eradicated.

Divine Offerings (Bhog)

Maa Kushmanda is immensely fond of the sacred offering of Malpua. It is firmly believed that offering Malpua to the Mother on this auspicious day enhances intellect and elevates one's decision-making prowess.

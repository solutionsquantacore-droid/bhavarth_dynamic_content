The 51 Shakti Peethas (Tantra Chudamani)

The 51 Authentic Shrines of Goddess Sati

Verified: The Shakti Peethas are significant shrines and pilgrimage destinations in Shaktism. According to the 'Tantra Chudamani' scripture, there are exactly 51 authentic Shakti peethas, representing the locations where body parts of the Goddess Sati fell to Earth. We have rigorously verified this list against the Tantra Chudamani for utmost accuracy.

1. Hinglaj (Kottari)

📍 Balochistan, Pakistan

Fallen Part: Brahmarandhra (Top of Head)

Bhairava: Bhimlochan

"An ancient and remote cave shrine in the Makran desert."

2. Karavira (Mahishmardini)

📍 Kolhapur, Maharashtra

Fallen Part: Eyes

Bhairava: Krodhish

"Famous as the Mahalaxmi Temple of Kolhapur."

3. Sugandha (Sunanda)

📍 Shikarpur, Bangladesh

Fallen Part: Nose

Bhairava: Trayambak

"Located on the banks of the Sunanda river."

4. Amarnath (Mahamaya)

📍 Pahalgam, Jammu & Kashmir

Fallen Part: Throat

Bhairava: Trisandhyeshwar

"Situated in the holy Amarnath ice cave."

5. Jwalamukhi (Siddhida)

📍 Kangra, Himachal Pradesh

Fallen Part: Tongue

Bhairava: Unmatta Bhairav

"Famous for the eternal flame that burns naturally from the rocks."

6. Jalandhar (Tripurmalini)

📍 Jalandhar, Punjab

Fallen Part: Left Breast

Bhairava: Bhishan

"A major spiritual center, also known as Devi Talab Mandir."

7. Vaidyanath (Jai Durga)

📍 Deoghar, Jharkhand

Fallen Part: Heart

Bhairava: Vaidyanath

"Co-located with the sacred Vaidyanath Jyotirlinga."

8. Guhyeshwari (Mahamaya)

📍 Kathmandu, Nepal

Fallen Part: Both Knees

Bhairava: Kapali

"Located near the revered Pashupatinath temple."

9. Manasa (Dakshayani)

📍 Tibet

Fallen Part: Right Hand

Bhairava: Amar

"Situated near the sacred Mount Kailash and Lake Manasarovar."

10. Viraja (Vimala)

📍 Puri, Odisha

Fallen Part: Navel

Bhairava: Jagannath

"Located within the complex of the world-famous Jagannath Temple."

11. Gandaki (Gandaki Chandi)

📍 Muktinath, Nepal

Fallen Part: Right Cheek

Bhairava: Chakrapani

"Situated near the sacred Gandaki river in the Himalayas."

12. Bahula (Bahula)

📍 Burdwan, West Bengal

Fallen Part: Left Arm

Bhairava: Bhiruk

"A prominent temple on the banks of the Ajay river."

13. Ujjaini (Mangal Chandika)

📍 Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh

Fallen Part: Elbow

Bhairava: Kapilambar

"Located in the ancient city of Ujjain (Harsiddhi Mata)."

14. Chattal (Bhavani)

📍 Sitakunda, Bangladesh

Fallen Part: Right Arm

Bhairava: Chandrashekhar

"Located on the Chandranath Hill in Chattogram."

15. Tripura (Tripura Sundari)

📍 Udaipur, Tripura

Fallen Part: Right Foot

Bhairava: Tripuresh

"A beautiful temple constructed in the traditional Bengali Ek-ratna style."

16. Trisrota (Bhraamari)

📍 Jalpaiguri, West Bengal

Fallen Part: Left Leg

"Situated amidst the lush tea gardens of North Bengal."

17. Kamakhya (Kamakhya)

📍 Guwahati, Assam

Fallen Part: Yoni (Genitals)

"The most powerful Tantric shrine, celebrating the divine feminine power."

18. Prayag (Lalita)

📍 Prayagraj, Uttar Pradesh

Fallen Part: Fingers

Bhairava: Bhava

"Located at the holy Triveni Sangam."

19. Jayanti (Jayanti)

📍 Baurbhag, Bangladesh

Fallen Part: Left Thigh

20. Yugadya (Bhoot Dhatri)

📍 Burdwan, West Bengal

Fallen Part: Right Great Toe

"The idol is traditionally submerged in a pond and brought out during Navratri."

21. Kalighat (Kalika)

📍 Kolkata, West Bengal

Fallen Part: Right Toes

Bhairava: Nakuleshwar

"One of the most famous and highly visited Shakti Peethas in India."

22. Kireet (Vimala)

📍 Murshidabad, West Bengal

Fallen Part: Crown

23. Varanasi (Vishalakshi)

📍 Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh

24. Kanyakumari (Sarvani)

📍 Kanyakumari, Tamil Nadu

Fallen Part: Back

"Located at the southernmost tip of India, dedicated to the virgin Goddess."

25. Kurukshetra (Savitri)

📍 Kurukshetra, Haryana

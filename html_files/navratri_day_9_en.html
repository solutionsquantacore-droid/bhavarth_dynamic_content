Maa Siddhidatri

Ninth Day of Navratri (Maha Navami)

The Divine Form of Maa Siddhidatri

On the ninth and final day of Navratri, 'Maa Siddhidatri' is worshipped. As the name suggests, she is the provider of all types of Siddhis (perfections and accomplishments) to her devotees. She is seated on a lotus flower and her mount is a lion. She has four arms, adorned with a mace, discus, drum (damaru), and a lotus flower.

Siddhagandharvayakshadyairasurairamarairapi.
Sevyamana Sada Bhuyat Siddhida Siddhidayini ||

Puja Vidhi & Havan

  • Bath & Worship: Wearing purple or violet clothes on the day of Maha Navami is considered highly auspicious.
  • Puja Items: Offer Kumkum, Akshat, and Champa or Lotus flowers to the Mother Goddess.
  • Havan: On the ninth day, Havan is performed while remembering all the forms of Navadurga, which marks the completion of the Navratri fast.
  • Kanya Pujan: On Navami day, there is a special tradition of worshipping and feeding young girls (Kanya Pujan).

Mythological Tale

According to the Markandeya Purana, there are eight main Siddhis—Anima, Mahima, Garima, Laghima, Prapti, Prakamya, Ishitva, and Vashitva. Maa Siddhidatri is capable of bestowing all these Siddhis upon her devotees.

According to mythology, Lord Shiva also attained all these Siddhis through severe penance only by the grace of Maa Siddhidatri. By Her immense grace, half of Lord Shiva's body became that of the Goddess, and He became famous in the world as 'Ardhanarishvara'. Worshipping Maa Siddhidatri fulfills all worldly and otherworldly desires.

Divine Offering (Bhog)

On Maha Navami, Maa Siddhidatri is specially offered Sesame seeds and delicacies made from it, as well as Halwa, Chana, and Puri. It is believed that this protects from untoward incidents and grants the blessing of success in life.

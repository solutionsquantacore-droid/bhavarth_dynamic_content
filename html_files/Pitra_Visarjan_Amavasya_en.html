Pitra Visarjan Amavasya

Sarvapitri Amavasya - The day of respectfully bidding farewell to ancestors

What is Sarvapitri Amavasya?

Pitra Visarjan Amavasya (also known as Sarvapitri or Mahalaya Amavasya) is the final and most significant day of the 16-day Pitru Paksha period. It is believed that during Pitru Paksha, our ancestors descend to Earth, and on the day of this Amavasya, they return to their realm (Pitru Loka). On this very day, they are respectfully bidden farewell by offering the final Shraddh and Tarpan.

Special Significance

According to the scriptures, if a person does not know the exact date (tithi) of their ancestors' demise, or for some reason was unable to perform the Shraddh on the prescribed date during Pitru Paksha, they can perform the Shraddh for all known and unknown ancestors on the day of Sarvapitri Amavasya. The Tarpan performed on this day liberates the family from Pitra Dosh and brings peace, happiness, and prosperity to the home.

DOs and Don'ts

DOs

Respectfully serve Satvik food (like kheer, puri, sabzi) to Brahmins.
Ensure to take out food for Panchbali (cow, dog, crow, ant, and deities).
In the evening, face the South direction and light a four-faced lamp (this is called Deep Daan).
Donate clothes or food grains to the needy and poor.

Don'ts

Strictly avoid consuming Tamasic food (meat, alcohol, onion, garlic).
Do not insult any beggar, sage, or animal arriving at your door (ancestors can arrive in any form).
Do not get angry and maintain peace in the house.
Avoid cutting hair or nails.

Puja & Farewell Vidhi

Take a bath in the morning and wear clean clothes (preferably white or yellow).
Facing the South direction, offer Tarpan to the ancestors using Kusha grass, black sesame seeds, and water.
To bid farewell to the ancestors, light a mustard oil lamp at the main door or on the terrace in the evening, illuminating their path back.
With folded hands, ask for forgiveness for all known and unknown mistakes and seek the blessings of your ancestors.

Special Remedies (Pitra Dosh Shanti)

On this day, offer water mixed with black sesame seeds at the roots of a Peepal tree and light a lamp beneath it.
Chant the mantra 'Om Pitrabhya Namah' or recite the 7th chapter of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita.

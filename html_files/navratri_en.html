Navratri Complete Guide

1. Kalash Sthapana (Ghatasthapana)

Kalash Sthapana is the most significant ritual during Navratri. It marks the beginning of the nine days of festivity.

  • Clean the puja room and spread a red cloth on a wooden seat.
  • Place a picture or idol of Goddess Durga.
  • Fill the Kalash (copper/brass pot) with clean water, Gangajal, a coin, and betel nut.
  • Place five mango leaves at the brim and top it with a coconut wrapped in a red cloth.

2. Akhand Jyoti (The Eternal Flame)

Lighting an Akhand Jyoti brings peace, prosperity, and blessings to the household.

  • The Jyot must be lit for all 9 days continuously.
  • Use pure cow ghee or sesame (til) oil.
  • Place the lamp on the right side of the Goddess if using ghee, or the left side if using oil.
  • Ensure it is protected from wind and do not leave the house completely empty.

3. Fasting Rules (Vrat Vidhi)

Fasting purifies the body and mind, helping you focus entirely on the Goddess.

  • Consume only Satvik food (e.g., Kuttu ka Atta, Sabudana, Fruits, Milk).
  • Strictly avoid onion, garlic, meat, and alcohol.
  • Maintain a calm mind, speak truthfully, and practice Brahmacharya (celibacy).

4. Daily Puja Vidhi

Worshiping the Goddess daily brings spiritual awakening.

  • Wake up early, take a bath, and wear clean clothes.
  • Offer fresh red flowers (like hibiscus), kumkum, and sweets to the Goddess.
  • Recite the Durga Chalisa and chant the Navarna Mantra: "Om Aim Hreem Kleem Chamundayai Vichche".
  • Perform the Aarti in the morning and evening with the family.

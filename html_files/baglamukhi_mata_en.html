Divine Pitambara Mahavidya • Eighth Supreme Goddess

The Sacred Katha of Maa Baglamukhi

Om Hleem Baglamukhyai Namah • Victory to Maa Pitambara

Maa Baglamukhi is revered as the eighth transcendental Mahavidya among the Ten Wisdom Goddesses of Sanatana Dharma. She is the supreme embodiment of divine shakti, courage, crisis dissolution, and the cosmic power of Stambhana—the immobilization of adverse forces and ill speech. Her sacred grace bestows unshakeable inner fortitude, truthful speech, and divine protection.

Part 1 Divine Form & Essence

1. Who is Maa Baglamukhi?

Maa Baglamukhi holds the revered position of the eighth Mahavidya among the Ten Great Wisdom Goddesses. She is affectionately worshipped as Pitambara Devi, the golden-complexioned Mother adorned in brilliant yellow silk garments and golden ornaments.

She is venerated as the goddess of supreme power, valor, invulnerable protection, and the divine force that stills and paralyzes (Stambhana) the malicious activities of hostile entities.

In Her classical iconography, Maa Baglamukhi holds a formidable golden mace (gada) in one hand, while with Her other hand She firmly grips the tongue of a demon. This sacred iconographic gesture symbolizes complete mastery and control over deceitful speech, falsehood, malevolent thoughts, and negative cosmic forces.

Her divine iconography delivers an eternal spiritual message: no matter how virulent or destructive untruth and wicked speech may appear, they are rendered entirely powerless before the radiant light of Divine Consciousness.
Part 2 Cosmic Manifestation

2. The Manifestation of Maa Baglamukhi

According to ancient Puranic accounts, during the Satya Yuga, a catastrophic cosmic tempest arose, threatening the complete destruction of the universe. Furious vortexes of gale-force winds and destructive energies threw the three worlds into chaos, and it appeared that cosmic order and equilibrium were doomed to collapse.

Deeply moved by the distress of the cosmos, Lord Vishnu undertook rigorous tapas (spiritual austerities) on the banks of Haridra Sarovar (the sacred turmeric lake) in the Saurashtra region to appease Adishakti.

Pleased by Lord Vishnu's selfless devotion and penance, the Supreme Mother manifested from the depths of the radiant golden waters of Haridra Sarovar in a blinding effulgence of pure yellow light as Maa Baglamukhi.

The very instant She manifested, the catastrophic cosmic storm was brought to an immediate standstill (Stambhana), and universal harmony was restored. Ever since, Maa Baglamukhi has been revered as the supreme savioress who arrests all perils and neutralizes negative energies.
Part 3 Spiritual Symbolism

3. The Mystery of Grasping the Asura's Tongue

In Her renowned posture, Maa Baglamukhi firmly holds the tongue of a demon in one hand while wielding a mace in the other. This visual depiction carries profound spiritual significance:

  • ✦Mastery of Speech (Vaani Sanyam): The Mother grants the power to silence deceit, harsh words, gossip, slander, and malicious intent.
  • ✦Stilling Negative Thoughts: She calms internal turmoil, silencing jealousy, anger, dread, and destructive mental patterns.
  • ✦Conquest of Injustice: She deprives oppressive forces of their strength, empowering the righteous to prevail without fear.

Devotees believe that Her divine grace infuses the human spirit with unyielding courage, allowing seekers to confront challenging crises with serene inner composure.

Part 4 Pitambara Form

4. Why is She Revered as Pitambara?

Maa Baglamukhi is revered as Pitambara Devi because of Her intimate, sacred connection with the color yellow (Peet Varna).

Every aspect of Her manifestation and worship is saturated in golden-yellow radiance:

  • 💛She is attired in golden-yellow robes and adorned with lustrous yellow ornaments.
  • 💛Her worship prominently features yellow marigold or oleander flowers, turmeric roots, yellow silk, and yellow sweets (such as besan laddoos and pedas).
  • 💛In Vedic philosophy, the color yellow symbolizes transcendental wisdom, solar energy, auspiciousness, purity, and spiritual enlightenment.
Part 5 The Dashamahavidyas

5. Position in the Ten Mahavidyas

In the Shakta tradition, the ten transcendental manifestations of the Supreme Goddess Adishakti are known as the Dashamahavidyas. Maa Baglamukhi presides as the eighth Mahavidya:

1. Maa Kali
2. Maa Tara
3. Maa Tripura Sundari
4. Maa Bhuvaneshwari
5. Maa Chhinnamasta
6. Maa Bhairavi
7. Maa Dhumavati
8. Maa Baglamukhi ★
9. Maa Matangi
10. Maa Kamala
Among these ten aspects of wisdom, Maa Baglamukhi is recognized as the sovereign mistress of 'Stambhana Shakti'—the cosmic capacity to arrest danger and bestow fearlessness upon Her seekers.
Part 6 Simple Worship Procedure

6. How to Worship Maa Baglamukhi

Householders can worship Maa Baglamukhi in the quiet sanctity of their homes with pure devotion, heartfelt reverence, and simplicity:

Sacred Puja Offerings (Samagri):

  • ✓Fresh yellow flowers (marigold, oleander) & clean yellow cloth
  • ✓Whole turmeric roots or ground turmeric & yellow-dyed akshat (rice)
  • ✓Pure ghee lamp (deepak) & aromatic incense (dhoop)
  • ✓Seasonal yellow fruits & yellow sweets (besan ladoo or peda)
  • ✓Pure consecrated water in a clean copper or brass vessel

Simple Step-by-Step Worship Vidhi:

1
Bathe in the morning and wear clean yellow or light-colored, sattvic attire.
2
Clean the sacred altar, face East or North, spread a clean yellow cloth, and reverently install the portrait or Yantra of Maa Baglamukhi.
3
Light the consecrated ghee lamp and incense to invite divine presence.
4
Offer turmeric, yellow akshat, fragrant yellow blossoms, fruits, and yellow sweets at Her lotus feet.
5
Sit in peaceful contemplation, meditating upon Her divine, luminous golden form.
6
Place your heartfelt prayers and righteous intentions before the compassionate Mother.
7
Conclude by singing Her sacred Aarti, seeking forgiveness for any errors, and praying for universal harmony.
Important Note: Complex tantric rituals, nyasa, shatkarmas, and rigorous esoteric sadhanas must strictly be undertaken only under the direct guidance of a qualified and realized spiritual Guru. General householders should embrace the pure path of sattvic bhakti.
Part 7 Sacred Mantra

7. Sacred Mantra of Maa Baglamukhi

For daily devotion, householders reverently recite this potent and auspicious mantra:

Maa Baglamukhi Saral Mantra
ॐ ह्लीं बगलामुख्यै नमः।
Om Hleem Baglamukhyai Namah ||
Meaning: Salutations to Goddess Baglamukhi, who dissolves all obstacles, dispels negative forces, stilling all falsehood and granting supreme protection and righteousness.

Devotees chant this mantra with a calm and pure mind, often using a turmeric (haldi) rosary. For complex esoteric mantras, guidance and initiation from a preceptor are indispensable.

Part 8 Sacred Shrines & Peethas

8. Prominent Temples of Maa Baglamukhi

Devotees from across the globe visit these renowned and spiritually charged shrines of Maa Baglamukhi in India:

🏛️
1. Pitambara Peeth – Datia, Madhya Pradesh
One of the most famous and spiritually potent Shakti Peethas in India, established by the revered ascetic Swami Ji Maharaj. It is a premier sanctuary of intense spiritual power and national reverence.
🏛️
2. Maa Baglamukhi Temple – Nalkheda, Madhya Pradesh
Situated on the serene banks of the Lakhundar river in Agar Malwa district, this ancient shrine traces its origins back to the Mahabharata era, where King Yudhishthira prayed for victory and righteousness.
🏛️
3. Maa Baglamukhi Temple – Bankhandi, Himachal Pradesh
Nestled in the picturesque foothills of Kangra Valley, this revered shrine is associated with the Pandavas during their exile, known to bestow instant fearlessness upon pilgrims.
Part 9 Spiritual Lessons

9. Spiritual Lessons from the Katha

The divine narrative of Maa Baglamukhi imparts vital life wisdom and ethical principles:

  • 1.Patience & Valor: Maintain serene composure, fortitude, and resilience even during severe worldly turbulence.
  • 2.Restraint of Speech: Guard your speech scrupulously; abstain from falsehood, malice, harsh speech, and idle disputes.
  • 3.Firmness Against Injustice: Stand unyielding before negativity, wrongdoing, and oppression with righteousness.
  • 4.The Path of Truth & Dharma: Walk steadfastly upon the path of integrity, virtue, and moral uprightness regardless of circumstances.
  • 5.Unwavering Faith: Cultivate deep, sincere faith in the Supreme Divine; righteousness ultimately triumphs over chaos.

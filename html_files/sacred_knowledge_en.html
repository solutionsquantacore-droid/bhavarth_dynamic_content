Sacred Scriptures & Knowledge

The Foundation of Sanatana Dharma

The knowledge of Hinduism (Sanatana Dharma) is vast and infinite. It rests upon a profound library of texts, philosophies, and rules of life laid down by ancient sages. Here is an outline of our most sacred scriptures and knowledge systems.

4 Vedas

The oldest and most authoritative Hindu scriptures.

  • Rigveda (Knowledge of Hymns)
  • Yajurveda (Knowledge of Liturgy)
  • Samaveda (Knowledge of Melodies)
  • Atharvaveda (Knowledge of Everyday Life)

10 Principal Upanishads

The philosophical core of Hinduism (Mukhya Upanishads).

  • Isha
  • Kena
  • Katha
  • Prashna
  • Mundaka
  • Mandukya
  • Taittiriya
  • Aitareya
  • Chandogya
  • Brihadaranyaka

6 Vedangas

Ancillary disciplines required for the understanding of the Vedas.

  • Shiksha (Phonetics)
  • Kalpa (Ritual Canon)
  • Vyakarana (Grammar)
  • Nirukta (Etymology)
  • Chhanda (Meter)
  • Jyotisha (Astronomy/Astrology)

6 Darshanas

The six orthodox schools of Hindu philosophy.

  • Samkhya (Rationalism)
  • Yoga (Discipline & Meditation)
  • Nyaya (Logic)
  • Vaisheshika (Atomism)
  • Mimamsa (Reflection/Exegesis)
  • Vedanta (Conclusion of Vedas)

4 Purusharthas

The four proper goals or aims of a human life.

  • Dharma (Righteousness/Duty)
  • Artha (Prosperity/Wealth)
  • Kama (Desire/Pleasure)
  • Moksha (Liberation/Salvation)

4 Ashramas

The four stages of human life.

  • Brahmacharya (Student Phase)
  • Grihastha (Householder Phase)
  • Vanaprastha (Retirement Phase)
  • Sannyasa (Renunciation Phase)

108 Upanishads

The Muktika canon lists 108 Upanishads spanning various traditions.

The list includes the 10 principal ones, plus 98 others dedicated to Vedanta, Yoga, Sannyasa, Shiva, Vishnu, and Shakti traditions. They contain the highest mystical wisdom of ancient seers.
